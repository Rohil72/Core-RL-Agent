# Q1 Publication Readiness

`[PARTIAL] [NEEDS-REPAIR]`

## Verdict

The project contains a credible applied-AI research contribution, but the
current evidence does not support submission as a robust profitable-trading
system to a reputable Q1 journal.

If submitted in its current state, architectural novelty could survive initial
screening, but the empirical claim would likely fail review because the sealed
confirmation was negative, the final observed period has been reused, and
several headline development results were affected by later-discovered
protocol defects.

## Research Contributions Already Present

- A Transformer representation that produces highly reproducible latent market
  states with future-outcome separation.
- A causal historical-memory architecture that grounds decisions in retrieved
  experiences rather than only a feed-forward head.
- Distributional evidence aggregation with downside tails, agreement,
  effective sample size, diversity, and interpretable trade evidence.
- A substantial ablation ladder covering outcome semantics, metric learning,
  exposure control, reliability, rally prototypes, neutral memory, adapters,
  offline RL, regional/global transfer, and multi-scale retrieval.
- A repeated cross-phase finding: sophisticated confidence and risk layers can
  improve drawdown while degrading transferable alpha.

These are more defensible contributions than any single Sharpe figure.

## Current Review-Blocking Evidence Gaps

1. The locked global/global confirmation returned `-6.02%` with Sharpe
   `-0.376`, while raw memory remained mildly positive.
2. Every available 2025 through 2026 Q1 result has been inspected and is no
   longer fresh confirmation.
3. The mixed timestamp-precision lookahead defect affects the interpretation of
   pre-fix Phase 4 causal-memory artifacts.
4. The final enriched-memory study used an unbounded path-quality term and had
   incomplete inference coverage.
5. Fixed liquid-company universes retain survivorship and researcher-selection
   bias.
6. Performance is concentrated by market and is not consistently better than
   equal-weight or momentum.
7. The study still lacks one locked benchmark table covering modern forecasting
   models, classical machine learning, raw-feature nearest neighbours, and
   simple market baselines under the exact same execution contract.

## Venue Fit

The architecture fits an applied intelligent-systems journal better than a
top finance journal. A journal such as Expert Systems with Applications
explicitly covers intelligent systems in finance and stock trading. The paper
would need to be framed around retrieval-grounded decision support,
transferability failure, and interpretable memory rather than a claim of
profitable autonomous trading.

A forecasting or finance venue would demand stronger point-in-time data,
constituent and delisting controls, factor-adjusted returns, economic
identification, and a genuinely untouched evaluation. Reproducible data and
program supplements are also expected in serious forecasting work.

## Minimum Evidence Before Submission

- Complete the frozen closing repair without adding variants.
- Freeze the final mechanism regardless of its result.
- Evaluate once on a genuinely untouched market universe or later live period.
- Use point-in-time membership where available and document survivorship where
  it is not.
- Compare against raw-feature k-NN, PCA k-NN, linear and tree models, momentum,
  equal-weight, and at least two strong time-series architectures.
- Report per-market and factor-adjusted alpha, block-bootstrap uncertainty,
  PBO/DSR, turnover, transaction-cost sensitivity, and concentration.
- Release code, immutable configs, seeds, manifests, and a reproducible data
  construction procedure.

Until those conditions are met, the honest claim is methodological:

> Retrieval-grounded market memory can expose useful latent opportunity
> structure and reduce catastrophic entries, but learned confidence and policy
> overlays frequently fail under temporal and geographic transfer.

That claim is research-worthy. It is not yet a Q1-validated trading result.
