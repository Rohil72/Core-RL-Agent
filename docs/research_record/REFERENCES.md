# Research References

This file preserves the papers explicitly tied to the implemented research
branches. It does not claim that the project reproduces their experiments.

## Representation and Forecasting

- Nie et al., [A Time Series is Worth 64 Words: Long-term Forecasting with
  Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14730), ICLR 2023. Relevant to the
  patch-based temporal encoder.

- [Retrieval Augmented Time Series Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04163), 2025.
- [Retrieval-Augmented Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08249), 2024.
